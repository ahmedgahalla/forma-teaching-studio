# 3.29 - Tooth chart layout, hover preview and lecture clarity

## Goal and before/after

The owner reported that the bottom tooth chart mixed with the central model, tooth selection was difficult to see, and lecture setup was unclear. This slice repairs those interaction and layout boundaries while retaining Claude's anatomy, diagram and Atlas appearance.

- The Explore stage previously reserved less height than its voice-enabled viewport required, with visible parent overflow. On a short screen, the child could paint through the following chart. The stage now owns a nonshrinking 290px minimum, its viewport fits that allocated row, and the workspace scrolls when needed. The chart stays a separate solid-surface sibling; the adjacent inspector scrolls within its row and hides at the existing narrow breakpoint.
- Hovering or keyboard-focusing a visible-arch tooth in the chart previews the corresponding 3D tooth. Leaving, blurring, changing model/arch or entering a lecture clears that transient preview. Hidden arches stay hidden until the existing explicit selection click. Preview does not select, pause playback, change voice references or add undo history.
- Hover and selected teeth use a stronger warm surface-and-edge glow. Selection takes priority over hover and natural enamel remains intact. Two scene-owned shared materials serve all teeth and are disposed on teardown; source geometry is borrowed and the render loop adds no allocations. The diagram uses matching warm preview and persistent selected states.
- Lecture entry says Choose lecture, with a short choose/review/present sequence. Review notes and Present replace ambiguous mode labels, with a visible step count and the existing named step selector. Mode changes explicitly explain that they restart the current prepared step. Presenter notes remain opt-in while presenting and excluded from the separate audience window.
- A compact step guide distinguishes static inspection, authored playback, activated mechanics experiments and temporary comparisons from actual step capabilities. The existing playback bar remains the only playback control. A question detour names the paused lecture and step beside Return to lecture. New visible labels also work through the existing local typed/voice command grammar; previous aliases remain supported.
