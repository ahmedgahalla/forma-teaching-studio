## Delivered scope and decisions

- **A - Voice HUD:** the canvas and tooth labels fill the entire viewport (`inset: 0`). The HUD overlays its bottom 20%, sized by the viewport container. `VOICE_HUD_SAFE_AREA` in `lecture-layout.ts` is the single value shared with camera math and the CSS custom property supplied by both viewport components. Narration remains 16-32 px and at most two visible lines; confirmations are smaller (14-20 px) with secondary Heard text. The indicator says "Listening · paused while Forma speaks". Live-region semantics, pointer-events none, focus behavior, result expiry, themes and reduced motion remain. The viewport minimum is 290 px to fit compact narration plus listening state inside the overlay. Passive captions hide during voice feedback; interactive pointed-target/mobile tools move above it.
- **B - Lecture framing:** fit/opening/presets solve camera distance and target for the actual displayed vertices in an asymmetric safe region above the bottom overlay. The lecture region is NDC y [-0.52, 0.92]: a 4% top margin, 4% gap above the 20% HUD, centered at y=0.2. Horizontal occupancy targets 78%; full anatomy height may constrain it. Actual visible gum vertices are included in ordinary full-model fits so the upper gingiva is not cropped in default/front views; focus and isolation omit unrelated gums. Cutaway retains its support bounds. Aspect-ratio changes use the same safe-area fit to move both distance and target, preserving relative zoom and user pan. Same-aspect resizes retain exact saved coordinates. Camera snapshot data and exact restoration are unchanged. No canvas height is removed, and user orbit/zoom may intentionally move anatomy behind a transient caption.
- **C — Camera movement:** preset, fit, focus and study-side requests use a 520 ms smoothstep orbit interpolation. Quaternion orientation, radius and target interpolation avoid crossing through the model on opposite-side changes. All interpolation vectors/quaternions/matrices are allocated once per viewer lifecycle, with no new per-frame interpolation allocations. New requests replace the current tween from its displayed pose; user orbit cancels it. Reduced motion applies the destination immediately, including a preference change during movement. Resize/scene rebuild finishes the destination before preserving/rescaling it. Render barriers and PNG requests wait for the final drawn frame. Exact snapshot restores remain instant and are held unchanged until user interaction; OrbitControls cannot nudge pole views on later idle frames. A same-geometry rebuild suppresses the study gate's first automatic fit so an existing study orbit survives.
- **D - Material clarity:** shared materials and display-only vertex shading give ivory crowns stronger cavity relief and subdued highlights, warmer/darker roots and less reflective gingiva. Exposure is 0.88 and environment intensity 0.55. Clinical Studio has a cooler light gradient; Midnight retains its backdrop/palette. Existing owners dispose the materials and display geometry; no assets or textures were added. Tooth study fits crown plus roots into the same bottom-safe-area model, with independent 70% width/76% available-height margins for labels. Its explanation card remains in a separate grid column or row.
- **E — DPR:** a lifecycle helper applies the initial device ratio and re-arms a resolution media query against each new raw ratio. Renderer/composer ratios are capped at 2; their existing setters resize the buffer, both composer targets and passes. Changes between two already-capped ratios re-arm observation without redundant target work. Teardown removes the listener and ignores queued callbacks.

No teaching action/schema, backend behavior, saved-camera data shape, GLB or JSON metadata contract changed. The synthetic teaching model is not clinically validated.

The review also identified a global-key crash in Studio (hold-to-talk already guarded its target). The existing workspace handler is extracted into `useWorkspaceKeys` and checks `event.target instanceof Element` before calling `closest`. Window/Document regressions cover workspace shortcuts and hold-to-talk, preserving editable/composition/active-state behavior.

## Skipped and follow-up

- **New baseline screenshots are required.** The orchestrator will capture both themes in case/workflow/study at 1600×900 and approximately 800×600, including active narration/results and roots, at DPR 1 and 2. Verify actual narration text size, all preset/side transitions, card/label visibility, prepared-case playback, braces workflow and a typed command. Move a window between displays to exercise live DPR updates and test reduced motion.
- Host reruns of the exact root format/lint/format-check/full-build commands remain required. The cache and tool configuration were not altered to conceal sandbox failures.
- The HUD is an overlay on the full canvas. Framing reserves its safe area regardless of current speech, so caption changes do not move the camera. Manual orbit/zoom can cross into that band, as explicitly accepted by the orchestrator. The former canvas/label `bottom: 22%` rules and reduced-canvas test aspects were removed.
- Ordinary fits now protect visible gingiva as well as teeth and roots. The 4% top margin is verified on actual upper-gum vertices in default and front views; no claim is made that all model shapes can fill 65-80% width while remaining fully visible.
- The explanation card already has its own grid region. Only the HUD is overlaid; camera math uses the full canvas inside that region.
- Clinical color, projected contrast and actual browser font/layout acceptance remain unverified. No clinical/educator review is implied.
